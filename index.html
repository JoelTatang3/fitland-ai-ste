How it works

From your profile to a ranked shortlist.

No black box. Every recommendation comes with a reason you can check for yourself.

Step 1

Tell us who you are

Answer a short questionnaire, or upload a CV — we extract language, education, and family details automatically.

Step 2

We match against real data

Your profile is scored against country-level data: cost of living, safety, climate, schooling, and immigration pathways.

Step 3

Get a ranked, explained shortlist

See your top countries with the specific reasons each one ranked where it did — not just a single score.
